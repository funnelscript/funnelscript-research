# AGENTS.md

> Operating constitution and scientific research gates for AI agents contributing to FunnelScript Research.

## Directives & Operating Invariants

### 1. The Iron Rule: Hypothesis-First & Research-Gated Engineering (MANDATORY)
**No claims, proofs, grammar modifications, or architectural definitions may be authored without prior falsifiable hypotheses and academic evidence anchors.**
- Every theoretical addition must cite canonical literature (System Dynamics, Multi-Agent Systems, Computational Economics, Bayesian MMM).
- Every claim must state its falsification boundary.

### 2. The Continuous Trajectory & Roadmap Synchronization Invariant (تحديث المسار دائمًا - MANDATORY)
**You MUST CONTINUOUSLY synchronize the research roadmap, philosophical debates, and architectural models whenever theoretical insights or paradigm shifts emerge.**
- When design decisions are agreed upon in conversation or review, immediately codify them into `DEBATES.md`, `paper/`, or `spec/`.
- Never leave theoretical agreements as transient chat ephemera. Ensure the research path is continuously up to date and physically recorded on disk.

### 3. Mathematical Rigor & Formal Completeness
- EBNF grammars must remain formally closed without dangling non-terminals or syntax truncation.
- Equations must be dimensionally consistent and bounded.
- State machines must define explicit initial, intermediate, and terminal absorbing states.

### 4. Traceability & Implementation Parity
- All research proposals and changes must map to runnable evaluation scenarios in `evals/` and verifiable implementations in `funnelscript/funnelscript`.
