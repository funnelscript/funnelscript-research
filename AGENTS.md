# AGENTS.md

> Operating constitution and scientific research gates for AI agents contributing to FunnelScript Research.

## Directives & Operating Invariants

### 1. The Iron Rule: Hypothesis-First & Research-Gated Engineering (MANDATORY)
**No claims, proofs, grammar modifications, or architectural definitions may be authored without prior falsifiable hypotheses and academic evidence anchors.**
- Every theoretical addition must cite canonical literature (System Dynamics, Multi-Agent Systems, Computational Economics, Bayesian MMM).
- Every claim must state its falsification boundary.

### 2. The Living Intellectual Trilogy & Continuous Trajectory Synchronization (MANDATORY)
**You MUST CONTINUOUSLY maintain, synchronize, and update the Living Intellectual Trilogy in every work session without exception.**
The research trajectory is anchored in three interconnected living files:
1. **`DEBATES.md` (Dialectical Ledger):** Captures active theoretical trade-offs, philosophical conflicts, and thesis-antithesis synthesis.
2. **`DECISIONS.md` (Canonical Decision Ledger):** Permanently freezes accepted Architecture Decision Records (ADRs). Every established principle (e.g. 4-layer stack, agent-entity subtyping, decoupled calibration contracts) is assigned an immutable ID (`DEC-XXX`) with context, rationale, and consequences.
3. **`OUT_OF_THE_BOX.md` (Frontier Speculation Ledger):** Incubates unconventional, non-linear, and speculative ideas revolving around the core vision (adversarial competitor AI, viral memetics, macroeconomic shock injection, zero-knowledge proofs). These serve as seed stock for future research hypotheses.

*Rule:* Never leave theoretical agreements as transient chat ephemera. Ensure that `DEBATES.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, `OUT_OF_THE_BOX.md`, `paper/`, and `spec/` continuously reflect verified progress.

### 3. Strict 100% English Language Mandate (MANDATORY)
**All research papers, LaTeX manuscripts, EBNF specifications, markdown documents, comments, and commit messages across both repositories MUST be authored strictly in 100% high-precision, technical English.**
- Never commit Arabic or other non-English text into repository files.
- Translate any verbal prompt concepts or colloquial discussion points into rigorous, canonical academic terminology.

### 4. Mathematical Rigor & Formal Completeness
- EBNF grammars must remain formally closed without dangling non-terminals or syntax truncation.
- Equations must be dimensionally consistent and bounded.
- State machines must define explicit initial, intermediate, and terminal absorbing states.

### 5. Traceability & Implementation Parity
- All research proposals and changes must map to runnable evaluation scenarios in `evals/` and verifiable implementations in `funnelscript/funnelscript`.
