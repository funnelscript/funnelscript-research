# FunnelScript — Marketing Plan (English)

> **Critical framing note:** Pitching all 8Ps and 8Cs from day one risks sounding theoretical. Nobody knows where to start in the first 10 minutes. The smart move: sell the big vision as a headline, enter the market through concrete scenarios that show how a pricing decision (Price), distribution model (Place), or sales team capacity (People & Process) collides with campaigns (Promotion) and cash flow — all at once.

---

## 1. Positioning

FunnelScript is the first language for running and stress-testing marketing strategies.

It turns a marketing plan from a static slide deck into a live system — one you can execute, adjust at the parameter level (pricing, distribution, product, operations, channels), and see the financial outcome of, before spending a single dollar in-market.

## 3. How FunnelScript Covers the Full 8Ps / 8Cs

The claim needs substance. Here's what the language actually models — and why that matters to a CMO:

- **Product & Customer Value** — simulates how product quality, repeat purchase rates, returns, and long-term retention affect each other
- **Price & Cost** — tests what a 10% price change, a bundle, or a subscription tier does to demand volume and net margin
- **Place & Convenience** — compares direct, branch, distributor, and platform channels; models distribution cost and time-to-customer per channel
- **Promotion & Communication** — aggregates offline, sponsorship, PR, and digital — and measures where they overlap and reinforce each other
- **People & Process** — sets realistic capacity limits on sales, support, and fulfillment teams, so you can see exactly when demand growth triggers a bottleneck and degrades the customer experience
- **Physical Evidence & Productivity** — calculates packaging cost, delivery experience, and service level against actual return per dollar spent

Digital advertising is one input here, not the product category.

## 4. Go-To-Market: 3 Phases

### Phase 1 — Days 1–30: Prove the concept through dissection

- Publish a weekly series called **Strategy Teardown**: take a real market scenario (new product launch, branch opening, pricing change), build its plan in FunnelScript, and show how one operational or pricing adjustment flips the outcome entirely
- Release 5 free templates covering full scenarios: consumer product launch, B2B company expansion, service pricing, e-commerce sale season

### Phase 2 — Days 31–60: Build community and direct experience

- Launch an interactive sandbox on the site — any marketing director can adjust 4–5 variables in a pre-built plan and see the result on-screen, no code required
- Run free workshops with agency and university marketing teams; let them run their real plans inside the session

### Phase 3 — Days 61–90: Scale through partnerships

- Partner program for consultants and agencies to adopt FunnelScript as their standard for strategy deliverables
- Publish documented case studies with real numbers: companies that caught a plan problem or avoided a bad launch through a simulation run before going to market

## 7. Four Content Angles

**1. The slide deck vs. the live system**

Most marketing plans die after the first month because they were written as static files — they don't move when market conditions change. With FunnelScript, you change any assumption and see the effect on the whole business in seconds.

**2. The 8Ps interact — that's the point**

Marketing is not a social ad campaign. A strong campaign with the wrong price, an overloaded sales team, or slow shipping brings the whole plan down. FunnelScript puts every element in one place so you catch the bottleneck before it happens.

**3. Test 50 adjustments before going to market — not after**

There's no reason to pay for trial and error with a live budget when you can run 50 parameter adjustments on the simulator in an hour and go to market with only the strongest version.

**4. One language for marketing, finance, and ops**

The CMO talks about awareness and demand. The CFO talks about cash and margin. The COO talks about capacity. FunnelScript translates the marketing plan into numbers every function can read and see the impact on their own work.
